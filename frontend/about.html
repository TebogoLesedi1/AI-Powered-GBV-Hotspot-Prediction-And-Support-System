About Us

SafeSouth Africa is an AI-powered Gender-Based Violence Hotspot Prediction and Support System. We combine machine learning with an intelligent chatbot to identify high-risk areas across South Africa and connect people to the help they need — in real time.

Our Mission

To predict GBV hotspot areas, classify location risk, and provide chatbot-based support alongside nearby resources — so that no one faces violence without knowing where to turn.

Our Vision

A South Africa where data, technology and community work together to break the silence around gender-based violence and make support reachable for every person.

Our Values

Privacy, dignity and safety come first. Our predictions are decision-support signals — never proof of danger — and our chatbot is a guide, not a replacement for professional or legal advice.

What We Do

Our system is built around three integrated components: a data layer, a prediction engine, and a chatbot module. Together they turn public crime and demographic data into clear, actionable insight for communities and support organisations.

01

Data Layer

We collect, clean and engineer public GBV, crime, demographic and location-related data from trusted South African sources.

02

Prediction Engine

K-Means clustering detects hotspots, while an XGBoost classifier labels each area as Low, Medium or High risk based on engineered features.

03

Chatbot Module

A grounded NLP assistant recognises intent, detects signals of distress, and recommends nearby police stations, clinics and NGOs.

Ethics & Limitations

GBV data is sensitive, and historical records can reflect underreporting and reporting bias rather than true incidence. We treat every prediction as advisory. We do not store sensitive personal data without consent, and we state clearly that our chatbot does not replace professional or legal advice.
